Privacy Policy for KowhaiGoods Auto-Pinner

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how KowhaiGoods ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our Pinterest application and content.

Information We Collect

From Pinterest API

When you interact with our pins or content on Pinterest, we may receive:

  • Public profile information visible on your Pinterest account
  • Engagement data (saves, clicks, impressions on our pins)
  • Aggregate analytics data provided by Pinterest's API

From Affiliate Links

When you click an affiliate link in our pins, you may be redirected to a third-party retailer (e.g., Amazon). We may receive a commission if you make a purchase, but we do not collect any payment or personal information from you — that is handled by the retailer directly.

How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Understand which content and products resonate with our audience
  • Improve the relevance of our pins and product recommendations
  • Comply with Pinterest's API terms and affiliate disclosure requirements
  • Analyze pin performance and optimize our content strategy

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal information
  • We do not use your data for targeted advertising beyond Pinterest's own platform targeting
  • We do not store payment information — all transactions occur on third-party retailer sites
  • We do not track you across other websites or apps

Third-Party Services

Our pins may link to third-party services including:

  • Amazon Associates — When you click an Amazon affiliate link, Amazon's Privacy Policy applies. We earn a commission on qualifying purchases at no additional cost to you.
  • Gumroad — When we link to Gumroad products, Gumroad's Privacy Policy applies.
  • Pinterest — Your use of Pinterest is governed by Pinterest's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

We retain analytics data provided by Pinterest's API for the purpose of content optimization. We do not store individual user data beyond what Pinterest's API provides directly.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of having your Pinterest interactions tracked by our app

To exercise these rights, contact us at kowhaigoods@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us
