How it works

  1. Email a photo of your menu (or your menu board) to kowhaigoods.nz@gmail.com — we'll build a free personalised preview of your actual menu first, no obligation.

Common questions

Can I see mine before I pay? Yes — email us your current menu (a phone photo is fine) and we'll build a free personalised preview of your actual menu. You only go ahead if you love it.

What if my menu changes? Email us the changes — updated within 2 business days, free in year 1 (included with the NZ$39/year hosting after). The QR code never changes.

Do customers need an app? No — the QR opens your menu in the phone browser, like any website.

After the first year? NZ$39/year if you want us to keep hosting it — or take the files and host anywhere, they're yours.

Do you take a cut of tips or orders? Never. The tip button goes straight to your own Stripe/bank link.
