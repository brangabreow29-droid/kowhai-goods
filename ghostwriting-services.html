FAQ

Questions about our founder content writing service

What is your AI ghostwriting service?

Our AI ghostwriting service blends professional human editing with AI drafting tools to produce consistent, on-brand LinkedIn posts, founder articles, newsletters, and thought leadership content for busy NZ and Australian founders.

Who is the LinkedIn ghostwriter Australia package for?

The LinkedIn ghostwriter Australia package is designed for founders, executives, consultants, and service-business owners who want to stay visible on LinkedIn without writing every post themselves.

How does the founder content writing service work?

Each month we collect your ideas, interviews, and key messages via a short intake form or async voice notes. We turn them into a queue of polished, publication-ready content matched to your tone and business goals.

Is the content written by AI or a human?

We use AI to accelerate first drafts and research, then a human writer edits, fact-checks, and refines every piece so it reads naturally and matches your voice. You always review before anything goes live.

Can I request edits or changes?

Yes. Every package includes at least one round of revisions per piece. Premium clients receive unlimited revisions and a dedicated content strategist.
