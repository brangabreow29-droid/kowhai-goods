Common Questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. You describe the outcome you want in plain English; I handle everything underneath. You get a walkthrough and plain-language documentation.

Where does it run?

Your choice. Most clients prefer it runs on my infrastructure (you just receive the results); self-hosting on your own gear is available if you want full control.

What about my data and privacy?

Credentials are stored encrypted, your data stays in the workflow, and NZ Privacy Act obligations are respected. Nothing is on-sold or shared. You can export or delete everything at any time.

What if the site or API I depend on changes?

Every build includes error detection — you're alerted when something breaks rather than silently losing data. The first 30 days of fixes are included; after that, maintenance is optional.

Is AI involved?

Where it genuinely helps — summarising, drafting, classifying. Not as a gimmick. Every AI step has deterministic fallbacks and you approve anything that goes out under your name.
