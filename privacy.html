Overview

Kowhai Goods ("we", "us") operates kowhaigoods.com, which showcases and links to digital products (Excel templates, PDF playbooks, and other downloadable tools) sold through third-party platforms such as Gumroad. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Analytics data: We use privacy-focused, cookieless analytics (Umami, self-hosted) to count page views and referrers. No cookies are set, no personal identifiers are collected, and visitors cannot be individually identified.
  • No direct purchases on this site: All transactions happen on the platform you buy from (e.g., Gumroad). We never receive or store your card details. The platform shares your email address with the seller for delivery of your purchase and receipt.
  • Email correspondence: If you contact us, we keep your message and reply address so we can respond, and delete them once the matter is resolved.

Third-party services

  • Gumroad — payment processing and digital delivery, governed by Gumroad's privacy policy.
  • Umami (self-hosted) — aggregate, anonymous traffic statistics only.
  • YouTube API — we use the YouTube API Services to operate our channel; usage follows the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube API terms.

Your rights

You can request a copy or deletion of any personal information we hold about you (limited to email correspondence and purchase-delivery emails from Gumroad) by contacting us at KowhaiGoods@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Data retention

Analytics data is retained in aggregate form with no personal identifiers. Correspondence is deleted once resolved. Purchase and delivery records are retained as required by tax law.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the current version.
